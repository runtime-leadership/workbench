## Walk Through the Ladder Together

### Manager's View

What generally changes between the current and target levels? Where is the engineer already demonstrating the target level? Where does the next step need more consistency, broader scope, stronger ownership, or a better opportunity to be visible?

Use examples from real work.

### Engineer's View

What aligns with the manager's view? Where do you disagree, or what context and evidence is missing? Where do you see the most useful opportunity to demonstrate the next level?
