# Engineering 1:1

> Part of [Workbench by Runtime Leadership](https://github.com/runtime-leadership/workbench). Use this template on its own or in a private Workbench created from the GitHub template.

Use this as a shared preparation menu and working record. It is not a script, and you do not need to complete every section in every meeting.

### Topics Either Person Can Add

- What would be useful to discuss?
- Is there a decision, concern, or unresolved issue that deserves time?
- What should we revisit from the last conversation?

### Engineer Preparation

These prompts are optional, not required homework.

- Where would input, context, support, or feedback help?
- What is making your work easier or harder?
- Is there work you want more or less of?
- Is anything affecting your motivation, confidence, energy, or growth?
- What are you seeing that your manager may not see?

### Manager Preparation

- What have I observed since our last conversation?
- Is there feedback I should not delay?
- What context, recognition, or decision does this person need?
- Have I followed through on previous commitments?
- Is there a pattern involving workload, relationships, motivation, performance, or growth that we should discuss?

## Choose the Conversation

Review the available topics together and choose the most valuable use of the time.

> We have a few topics here. Which one would make this conversation most useful today?

Start with the engineer's priorities, then add the manager's observations, feedback, or context. Check a longer-running thread only when it is useful.

## Dedicated Goal Review

When goals need more than a regular check-in, reserve a conversation for them instead of squeezing them into the last few minutes.

- Do these goals still reflect the engineer's development priorities?
- Has the work or organizational context changed?
- What progress, evidence, or feedback have we seen?
- What has made progress difficult?
- Should a goal continue, change, be replaced, or be retired?
- What upcoming work could provide meaningful practice?
- What support, feedback, access, or sponsorship does the manager need to provide?

For a deeper level and growth discussion, use the [Engineering Level Growth Conversation Template](../engineering-level-growth-conversation/template.md).

## Periodic 1:1 Check

- Are these conversations useful?
- What should we do more or less of?
- Are we discussing the right things?
- Is anything difficult to raise here?
- Should we change the frequency, preparation, or format?

## Privacy

Keep concise shared notes rather than a transcript. Do not record or automatically transcribe a 1:1 by default. Follow company policy and applicable law, and avoid capturing sensitive information that belongs in an approved HR or company system.
