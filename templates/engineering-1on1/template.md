## Prepare Together

### Engineer's Topics

- Where would input, context, support, or feedback help?
- What is making the work easier or harder?
- Is there anything affecting motivation, energy, or growth?

### Manager's Topics

- What feedback, context, recognition, or decision should not wait?
- Is there a pattern involving workload, relationships, performance, or growth to discuss?
- What commitment from the previous conversation needs follow-through?
